Brand Story

Where the pause
becomes purpose.

Golf gives you four hours and eighteen holes to think. That space between shots — the walk down the fairway, the stillness before a putt — is rare in modern life. Selah was born in that space.

It's a Hebrew word from the Psalms. It means pause and reflect. We thought that was fitting for a golf brand. And we thought it was fitting for a life of faith. The two belong together.
